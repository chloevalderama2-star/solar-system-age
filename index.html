Your Age in the Solar System

Have you ever wondered how old you would be on another world? Enter your birthday and discover your age across the eight planets and Pluto.

Student: KATE CHLOE M. VALDERAMA Course & Section: BSIT - 1C

Your birthday is used to calculate how many Earth days you have lived.

Choose a World to Explore

Click any world to discover facts about it. Enter your birthday to calculate your age on each world.

How does the calculator work? A planet's year is the time it takes to complete one orbit around the Sun. Each world has a different orbital period, so your age in planetary years changes from world to world.

Planetary year lengths are approximate. Pluto is included as the ninth world for this project, but it is classified as a dwarf planet.
